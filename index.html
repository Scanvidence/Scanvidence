System Architecture

6-Stage Diagnostic & Radiogenomic Pipeline

From multi-sequence MRI ingestion to automated institutional report dispatch.

STAGE 01 ACQUISITION

Multi-Parametric MRI Ingestion

Standardized pre-processing of 4 core neuro-MRI modalities (T1w, T1c, T2w, T2-FLAIR) with rigid registration and intensity z-score normalization.

STAGE 02 3D SEGMENTATION

3D Deep Volumetric Vision

SegResNetB0 architecture with autoencoder regularization loss performing sliding-window multi-class sub-region delineation.

STAGE 03 RADIOMICS

Standardized IBSI Radiomics

High-throughput extraction of 107 mathematically standardized shape, first-order intensity, and second-order GLCM texture biomarkers.

STAGE 04 QUANTUM HAMILTONIAN

QUBO Combinatorial Optimization

Ising Hamiltonian feature correlation minimization executed on the 156-qubit IBM Heron processor to extract orthogonal biomarkers.

STAGE 05 QSVM KERNEL

Quantum Hilbert Space Embedding

9-qubit ZZFeatureMap encoding continuous radiomic vectors into a 512-dimensional complex Hilbert space for non-Euclidean fidelity estimation.

STAGE 06 ABDM M2 DELIVERY

Clinical Triage & FHIR Export

Automated routing of urgent high-risk cases to tertiary neuro-oncology boards with cryptographic NRCES FHIR diagnostic bundles.
